How it started

It starts with going outside

It started with early mornings at Lake Merritt — a sketchbook, bad coffee from a thermos, and a black-crowned night heron that would stand on the same rock every single dawn.

Oakland has some of the most overlooked wildlife of any American city. Pelicans gliding over the estuary. A white raccoon — genuinely white, like something out of a myth — spotted near the bird sanctuary. Cormorants spreading their wings on the rocks like dark angels. Coots charging anyone who gets too close to their turf.

We started drawing because we were in love with these places. Then came San Francisco — a city with weather unlike anywhere on earth. The fog rolling under the Golden Gate. The absurdity of wearing a jacket in August while tourists shiver. The way every neighborhood has its own private microclimate, its own mood.

We started making stickers because we wanted them on our laptops, water bottles, and cars. Other people wanted them too.

How we work

From sketchbook to sticker

Go outside, observe

Every design starts at the lake or in the field — watching, sketching, photographing. We want to catch the animal doing something real.

Draw it by hand

Pencil first, always. We work out the pose, the character, the feeling before anything goes digital. The sketch stage is where the soul lives.

Colour & refine

The sketch becomes a vector. We test colours, adjust contrast, and make sure the design reads beautifully at any size from 3 cm to poster.

Print, cut, ship

We work with quality small-batch printers. Every order is packed by hand with care — because a good unboxing is part of the experience.
