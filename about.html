How it started

A souvenir from everywhere,
for everywhere

It started with a suitcase full of postcards, market finds from Barcelona, and a sketchbook that never seemed to have enough pages.

We noticed that the most memorable souvenirs weren't the mass-produced ones — they were the ones that felt handmade, considered, a little unexpected. A pressed-flower postcard from a tiny Marseille shop. A cobalt tile magnet from a Lisbon ceramicist. Things that carried a place inside them.

So we started making our own. Designs rooted in the colours and patterns of the Mediterranean — terracotta and tile blue, golden afternoon light, the kind of red you only see on a flamenco dress or a Catalan door.

Today, Pétale Studio is a design catalog for people who collect beautiful little things — and for businesses, events, and creators who want to give something truly worth keeping.

Design principles

Art in the everyday

The best design doesn't hang on a wall — it lives on a laptop, a journal cover, a fridge door. We make art for real life.

Every palette is deliberate. We draw from Mediterranean traditions — Spanish azulejos, French poster art, Moorish geometry — to make colours that feel alive.

Weatherproof vinyl, archival inks, quality card stock. We make things meant to last — not disposable, but cherished.

Custom is personal

The best gifts are specific. We love working on custom orders — your event, your brand, your idea. Let's make it together.

How we work

From idea to in your hands

Sketch & Concept

Every design begins by hand — pencil on paper, playing with form, colour, and feeling before anything touches a screen.

Refine Digitally

The sketch becomes a vector. We test colours, adjust proportions, and make sure it looks as good at 3 cm as it does on screen.

Print & Cut

We work with small-batch printers who care about quality as much as we do. Die-cut, kiss-cut, or shaped — always sharp, always vibrant.

Packed with Care

Orders are packaged by hand. We add a little something extra — because a good unboxing is part of the experience.
