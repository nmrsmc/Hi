Common questions

FAQ

Do you ship internationally?

Yes! We ship worldwide. Shipping times and costs vary by destination — we'll include details in your quote.

Can I use my own artwork?

Absolutely. Send us your file (vector preferred, 300 dpi minimum) and we'll handle the rest. We can also help with design if needed.

What's the minimum order?

Catalog designs have no minimum — order just one. Custom designs typically have a minimum of 25–50 units depending on the product.

Are the stickers waterproof?

Yes. We use premium weatherproof vinyl with UV-resistant laminate. They'll survive bottles, laptops, and outdoor use.

Can I get a sample before ordering in bulk?

Yes — sample packs are available for bulk orders. Ask about it in your enquiry and we'll include it in the quote.

How do I pay?

We accept bank transfer, PayPal, and most major cards. Payment details are included in the invoice after your order is confirmed.
