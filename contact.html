Common questions

FAQ

Do you ship outside the Bay Area?

Yes! We ship anywhere in the US, and internationally too. Flat rate shipping — details included in your quote. Bay Area pickup is always an option.

Can I commission a specific Oakland creature?

Absolutely. We love custom wildlife commissions. Share a reference photo or description and we'll design it from scratch. Minimum order for custom designs is 25 units.

What’s the minimum order for catalog items?

No minimum for catalog stickers — order just one. Custom and bulk orders start at 25 units; pricing scales down significantly for larger runs.

Are the stickers actually weatherproof?

Yes — premium weatherproof vinyl with UV-resistant laminate. They handle water bottles, bike helmets, and Bay Area fog. We put them through it ourselves before we sell them.

Do you do wholesale for shops?

Yes! We're actively building wholesale partnerships with Oakland-area gift shops, bookstores, and nature centers. Get in touch with "wholesale" in your message.

How do I pay?

Venmo, Zelle, PayPal, or card via invoice. 50% deposit on custom orders, full payment on catalog orders. Details in your quote.
